1.Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro (born February 5, 1985) is a famousPortuguese football player, team captain for Al-Nassr and the Portugal national team, and winner of five Ballon d'Or awards.

2.Neymar

Neymar da Silva Santos Júnior (born February 5, 1992) is a famous Brazilian football player known for playing for Santos FC, FC Barcelona, and the Brazil national team. He is widely known for his incredible dribbling, speed, and scoring skills, and he is Brazil's all-time top goalscorer

3.Kylian Mbappé

Kylian Mbappé is a famous French professional football player born on December 20, 1998. He plays as a forward for La Liga club Real Madrid and captains the France national football team.

4.Erling Haaland

Erling Haaland is a world-famous Norwegian professional footballer, born on July 21, 2000, in Leeds, England. He plays as a star striker for the Premier League club Manchester City and the Norway national team.
